# The 3D forms: a gallery

**Takeaway.** Each ready-made form in `video/forms3d/` is one line to use: this film shows them all, one a paragraph.

**Viewer.** Someone about to build a film with the 3D point layer who wants to see what is on the shelf.

**Questions the film answers, in order.** What can the points become without writing a form? What does each form look
like, and what can it do?

The `> ` lines are the narration, exactly as spoken. The table under each paragraph is its beat sheet.

## 1. Text, globe, scatter, core

> Thirty thousand points can spell a word. They can rest as a turning globe, scatter into a cloud, or fall into one small core.

| on the words | change | then on screen |
|---|---|---|
| (start) | HOLD the points spelling "forms3d" | title |
| a turning globe | CHANGE the points to the globe | globe, label |
| scatter into a cloud | CHANGE the points to the scatter | scatter, label |
| one small core | CHANGE the points to the core | core, label |

## 2. Clusters

> Sheets stack the points in layers, like a network. A pass of light can climb through them.

## 4. Lorenz

> The Lorenz form integrates three equations and lets the points stream along the solution. Two markers can ride it and drift apart.

| on the words | change | then on screen |
|---|---|---|
| The Lorenz form | CHANGE the points to the flowing attractor | heading, attractor |
| Two markers | ADD two markers riding the solution | heading, attractor, markers |
| drift apart | HIGHLIGHT the markers on different wings | heading, attractor, markers |

## 5. Grid

> The grid gives every point a cell. Each cell takes a state, and a wave can flip them from one state to another.

| on the words | change | then on screen |
|---|---|---|

| on the words | change | then on screen |

## 7. Phyllotaxis

> Phyllotaxis places each seed by the golden angle. The head grows outward, then curls up and opens as a flower.

|---|---|---|

## 8. Waveform

> The waveform lines the points up as a sound's loudness over time. One colour marks the voice, another the music, and a dot marks now.

| on the words | change | then on screen |
|---|---|---|
| The waveform lines | CHANGE the points to a waveform | heading, waveform |
| marks the voice | HIGHLIGHT the voiced spans in the accent colour | heading, two-colour waveform, labels |
| a dot marks now | ADD a moving dot at the current time | heading, waveform, labels, now dot |
