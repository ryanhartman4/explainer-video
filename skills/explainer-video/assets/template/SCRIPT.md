# TITLE OF THE FILM

**Takeaway.** One sentence the viewer should be able to say after watching once. If it needs "and", it is two films.

**Viewer.** Who they are, what they already know, what they have never seen.

**Questions the film answers, in order.** The questions this viewer would ask, each one raised by the answer before
it. Each becomes a paragraph below.

The `> ` lines are the narration, exactly as it will be spoken. The table under each paragraph is its beat sheet: one
row for each change on screen, tied to the words being said when it happens. Check with `node tools/script_check.mjs`.

## 1. Title of the first paragraph

> Write the narration here. Short sentences, written for the ear. Open on something the viewer can picture.

| on the words | change | then on screen |
|---|---|---|
| (start) | HOLD the opening frame: the question | question |
| written for the ear | ADD the line under the question | question, line |
| something the viewer can picture | ADD that thing | question, line, thing |

## 2. Title of the second paragraph

> One paragraph is one idea, about ten to twenty-five seconds long. Copy each cue phrase from the narration exactly.

| on the words | change | then on screen |
|---|---|---|
| One paragraph is one idea | REPLACE the question with the heading | heading, thing |
| twenty-five seconds | COUNT the seconds up to 25 | heading, thing, count |
| exactly | HIGHLIGHT the thing | heading, thing, count |
